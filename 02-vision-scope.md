2. Vision and Scope (First Draft)
2.1 How This Project Was Acquired

This is client-driven, not market-driven — acquired through a city RFP, not a product launch. A mid-size city transportation department issued an RFP after a downtown parking study showed drivers circling for several minutes per trip while nearby garages sat under capacity. Software Corp's proposal centered on one shared backend connecting driver visibility and operator management, rather than the two disconnected products competing vendors pitched, and won the contract on that basis. Revenue is milestone-based against contracted deliverables, not recurring subscriptions.

2.2 About Software Corp

A fictitious software studio operating with unlimited theoretical budget, structured around six teams: Mobile Application, Web Application, Backend/API, Mapping & Location Services, Payment Integration, and QA/Testing — enough to take the project end to end.

2.3 Project Overview

A web-based and mobile solution that allows drivers to find, book, and pay for parking spaces in real-time, along with a dashboard to allow operators to manage prices, occupancy rates, and reporting of the same. The solution is able to integrate with third-party payments and navigation systems; must be in compliance with city ordinances.

2.4 Key Risks

Since this is a fixed-scope client project, not a product with market risk, the relevant risks are scope creep, budget overruns against the contract, missed milestones, and client dissatisfaction — mitigated through change control on new feature requests, clear milestone contracts, and weekly stakeholder check-ins.
